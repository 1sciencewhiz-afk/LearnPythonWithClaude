Safety and privacy

Written for learners and for the adults who look after them.

What we store

  • Your username, email address and a one-way hash of your password.
  • Your birth year only - never a full date of birth.
  • A guardian email, if you are under 13.
  • Which lessons you finished, your XP, streak and badges.
  • Your most recent code for each lesson, so you can come back to it.

What we do not do

  • No adverts and no third-party tracking scripts.
  • No chat, comments, messaging or profiles other learners can see.
  • No file uploads and no way to share code with strangers.
  • No selling or sharing of anyone's data.

Younger learners

Accounts are for ages 10 to 18. If you are under 13 the sign-up form asks for a parent or guardian email address, and we expect you to have their permission before you create the account. Depending on where this site is running, laws such as COPPA in the United States or the UK Age Appropriate Design Code may require verifiable parental consent - an operator of a public deployment is responsible for meeting those rules.

Running code safely

Your Python runs on the server in a locked-down child process: a throw-away folder, a stripped environment, hard limits on time, memory and output, and no access to files, the network or the system. A loop that never ends is stopped after a few seconds and reported back to you as a friendly message.

Deleting your account

Go to your account page and use "Delete your account". Everything - progress, saved code and badges - is removed from the database immediately.
